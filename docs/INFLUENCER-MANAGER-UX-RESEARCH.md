# Influencer manager workflow — UX research notes

## Recommended operating flow

1. Define the campaign objective, audience and one primary KPI.
2. Shortlist creators using audience fit, content fit, brand safety and credible performance evidence.
3. Define the deliverable package before comparing fees.
4. Negotiate commercial terms, including rights, exclusivity, revisions, timing and payment.
5. Keep a human approval gate before agreement and contracting.
6. Turn the agreement and brief into checkable production, disclosure and approval requirements.
7. Publish, capture the primary KPI, reconcile payment and use settled results to improve benchmarks.

## Information hierarchy used in the app

- **Always visible:** workflow intent, creator, platform, campaign outcome and deliverables.
- **Visible when evaluating:** one reliable evidence source, the recommendation, negotiation range and next action.
- **Progressively disclosed:** contact/program exceptions, usage rights, whitelisting, exclusivity and campaign-specific pricing overrides.
- **Results:** the primary KPI first, views second for CPM calibration, all other diagnostics on demand.

This hierarchy avoids treating an awareness collaboration like a conversion campaign and
prevents a long intake form from implying that every field is equally important.

## Sources

- FTC, [Endorsements, Influencers, and Reviews](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews) and [Endorsement Guides Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking): disclosure instructions and advertiser monitoring belong in the operating process, not as an afterthought.
- ANA, [Influencer Marketing Measurement Guidelines](https://www.ana.net/content/show/id/74274): measurement starts from the campaign objective, with consistent definitions across awareness, engagement and conversion goals.
- IAB Perú, [Guía para la contratación de influencers 2026](https://iabperu.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Guia_Contratacion_Influencers_IAB-2.html): the practical sequence is brief/co-creation, production and review, publication and reporting; agreements should specify deliverables, dates, approvals, payment, usage, exclusivity and disclosure.
- IAB Chile, [Guía práctica de Influencer Marketing](https://www.iab.cl/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Guia-Practica-de-Influencer-Marketing_b.pdf): creator selection should balance audience and content fit, brand safety and cost versus expected result, with detailed deliverables and reporting.
